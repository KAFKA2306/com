# Recurring-service model

A recurring service exists to support a repeated user decision or obligation. It is not justified by the mere existence of a cron expression or workflow file.

Before activation, define:

- user decision supported;
- target repositories and external surfaces;
- cadence or condition and timezone;
- freshness and primary-source policy;
- executor and required permission class;
- expected successful result;
- silent/no-notification behavior;
- retry and incident thresholds;
- privacy and cost limits;
- pause, review, and retirement conditions.

Register only observed, useful services. Historical automation remains unregistered until audited.
