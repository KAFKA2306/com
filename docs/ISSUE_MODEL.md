# GitHub issue model

## Directive

A bounded change, migration, audit, or investigation. It ends when its acceptance criteria have evidence or it is cancelled.

## Recurring service

A persistent responsibility with cadence or condition, executor, evidence policy, failure handling, and retirement criteria. Runs do not each require a new directive unless implementation changes.

## Incident

An observed failure or incorrect result. It records impact, evidence, containment, cause, recovery, and prevention work.

## Decision

A durable architectural or governance choice with context, alternatives, evidence, consequences, and review date.

Target-repository issues may hold product-specific discussion. The `com` parent retains portfolio intent and cross-repository completion.
